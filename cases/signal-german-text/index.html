Will It Clip?

When a German privacy notice loses its last two words

In Signal for iOS 7.75, the German nickname dialog on an iPhone 15 Pro cut off the part that told people who could see their nicknames and notes. The text stopped mid-sentence without an ellipsis.

Visible ending
Sie sind nur für
Expected ending
Sie sind nur für dich sichtbar.

The full sentence means “Only you can see them.” To check a hard cut-off like this, compare the selected lines with the complete translation. The example opens with both the text and the screenshot region filled in.
